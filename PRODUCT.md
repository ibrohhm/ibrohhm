# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and peer engineers evaluating the site owner professionally. They land via a shared link (resume, LinkedIn, article share) or search. Context: quick eval — skimming, not deep research on first visit.

## Product Purpose

Personal site for a software engineer: portfolio + technical writing. Homepage establishes credibility and who the person is; articles serve as proof of expertise and are also shared/read standalone (e.g. via search or dev.to cross-posts). Success = a visitor forms a positive, memorable impression and can find/read an article without friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, personal, approachable. Should read as a person, not a corporate resume — voice over polish-for-polish's-sake. Still credible: warmth doesn't mean unpolished or unserious.

## Anti-references

No named anti-references given. Avoid generic SaaS/corporate portfolio templates (stock hero + icon-grid "skills" cards) — those read as impersonal, working against the warm/personal goal.

## Design Principles

- Person over template — favor choices that reveal personality (voice, small human details) over generic professional polish.
- Content is the credibility — articles and their readability matter more than decorative flourish.
- Quick first impression — homepage should communicate who/what fast, since visitors are skimming.
- Shareable articles stand alone — article pages must read well arriving cold (via search/share), not just as part of a browsed site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline. No additional known user needs specified.
